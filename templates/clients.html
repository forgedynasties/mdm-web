How a build gets here

Builds are published by CI, not by hand: the agent's pipeline runs its tests, signs the APK with the key for that variant, and posts it to the slot. Publishing an unsigned or untested build is the failure this is meant to prevent, so there is no upload button. The firmware client is published from the build machine, because only it holds the platform keys.

POST {{.ServerURL}}/api/v1/agent-apk?slot=<slot>
X-API-Key: <admin key>
Content-Type: application/vnd.android.package-archive
<the signed APK>
