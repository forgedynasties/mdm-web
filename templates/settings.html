How metering works. Geolocation resolves a WiFi scan to coordinates (1-hour exact-scan cache + the learned index below + one call / 10 s server-wide cooldown). Geocoding turns coordinates into a street address (6-hour cache, ~11 m precision). Maps Embed is the browser map — free and unlimited; the count is device-page renders that showed the map. Request counters are in-memory and reset on restart; the learned index is persisted in the database.

Learned WiFi location index

persisted · avoids Google for known areas
Every successful Geolocation lookup teaches the server which access points (BSSIDs) live at which point. A later scan that overlaps ≥3 known APs (and ≥50% of the scan) is located locally — no Google call. Entries expire after 14 days. This is what drives the index hits above.
