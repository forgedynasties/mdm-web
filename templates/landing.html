What it does

One place for the whole lifecycle of a device: from the day it is unboxed to the firmware it runs a year later.

Fleet health, per venue

A score built from our own telemetry: battery, temperature, memory, Wi-Fi, crashes, charging pads. Worst venue first, with the reasons.

Geolocation

Position resolved from Wi-Fi scans. No GPS, works indoors, every device on a map clustered by restaurant.

Remote control

Live screen, taps and keys from the browser, wherever the device is. Shell, screenshot and logs when you need to dig.

OTA updates

Ship whole builds, not just apps. A/B seamless updates with per-device progress, reboot policy and a rollback slot.

Bulk actions

Install, uninstall, reboot, kiosk lock. Target a venue, a group or a pasted serial list. Every device acknowledges live.

Alerts that say what to do

Plain-language alerts with the fix, a daily briefing for the ops team, and filters that answer the question you actually have.

Why not buy one?

Off-the-shelf MDMs manage generic Android and charge per seat. Ours knows our hardware and ships our firmware.

Off-the-shelf MDM

  • Per-device, per-month fees
  • Generic Android, no idea what a charging pad is
  • Manages apps, not firmware

AIO MDM
