How it works

Everything for one build lives on its page — write the changelog, attach the OTA, publish, and push, without leaving. The device list is auto-scoped to the release's product (T7); kiosk devices are shown greyed as "wrong product", never pushable.

Best when

You think per release — "get this build out." Kills pains 1 & 3: zero page hops from draft to shipped.

Trade-off

The release page gets denser. Rollout history moves to a small "Deployments" list linked from here.

How it works

A short, explicit path — Release → Targets → Review. Each step is one decision, wrong-product and up-to-date devices are filtered out for you, and the summary shows exactly what will happen before you commit.

Best when

Pushing is occasional and you want no surprises. Kills pain 2: targeting is the whole middle step, nothing else competing for attention.

Trade-off

An extra click or two for power users who push often. Deep-links from a release jump straight to step 2.

How it works

Never leave the console. Product chips filter the left rail; click a release to see it on the right with a one-click Push OTA and a live rollout table that fills in as devices report. Manage (changelog/packages) opens from the same header.

Best when

You watch fleets and push often. Kills pain 3: one screen for browse → push → watch, no navigation at all.

Trade-off

Densest of the three; best on a wide screen. On mobile the rail collapses to a top dropdown.
