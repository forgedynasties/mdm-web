Fleet cards for a mixed fleet

T7s, kiosks, TV dongles, handhelds, counter terminals, tablets and phones, on three kinds of agent. A type icon leads every card so the fleet reads at a glance; a few layouts to choose from.

Online / Offline for firmware and DPC (live connection). Reporting / Not reporting for MDM-lite (check-ins only).

Layouts

A · Row cards

Today's list, led by the device: the type icon takes the battery chip's place and power joins the vitals, so a mains dongle and a T7 on its pad line up.

A4 · Aligned columns

The card keeps its shape, but every value sits in a column that lines up down the list. No chips: plain figures with a thin level bar, colour only when something is off.

DevicePowerTempMemoryStatusNeeds attention

A5 · Summary line

Two quiet lines per device, like an inbox: who and how it is doing on top, the vitals as plain text underneath, the one thing wrong at the end.

B · Tile grid

Every device a tile: icon and status first, vitals in one row. Suits a restaurant's handful of devices.

C · Compact list

One line per device for hundreds of devices; columns line up so a sort by temperature or battery reads down the page.
