Manage: kiosk as named policies

Not "pick devices, set a state, send" — define a policy once, assign it, and it stays a real thing you can see, edit, duplicate or delete. Matches how Intune/Jamf model this (configuration profiles), and scales cleanly once Charging pad and others join as policy types.

What changed from the dialog demo: every assignment is now a named row on the page itself, not a one-time action that vanishes into device state. "Enable kiosk on N devices" becomes "Save policy" — you're authoring a durable object, not firing a command. A dashed "Default" card always sits at the bottom for anything nothing else claims.
