Manage: better than a flat device list

The current table is one row per device — with kiosk config mostly uniform across a fleet (a handful of distinct states, applied at the restaurant/group level), that means paging through hundreds of near-identical rows to find the few that matter. Three ways to show state instead of rows.

Collapse identical devices into one card. Every device with the same kiosk state becomes one row, not many — "47 devices, Unlocked" instead of 47 identical lines. Expand a card to see (or search) its member devices; "Apply to more" pulls the same devices straight into the edit modal, pre-scoped.
