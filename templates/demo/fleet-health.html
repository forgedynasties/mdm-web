Direction C

Signal Heatmap

The most information-dense at-a-glance view. Rows are restaurants, columns are signals; every cell is color-coded ok / watch / critical with its value, so a bad restaurant×signal jumps out instantly — you read where and what in one pass. Click any hot cell to open the evidence behind it. Best when you have many venues.
