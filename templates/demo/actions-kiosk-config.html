Kiosk: from command to configuration

Right now Kiosk mode lives in the Actions page as a seventh command type — pick it, choose a target, hit Send, and it drops into the same queue as a one-shot Screenshot or Reboot. But kiosk isn't a one-shot action: it's a standing state a device sits in until someone changes it. Two ways to make the UI say that — Direction B goes further: a dedicated Manage page, not a Kiosk-only one, replacing the now-redundant Fleet Health dock slot.

Smallest change. Kiosk stays on the Actions page — same place people already look — but it's pulled out of the command-type row entirely and given its own card below, styled like a settings panel instead of a compose sheet. No "Send" button, no target picker step; you edit the scope you're already looking at and hit Save.

Bigger change, cleaner mental model — and it doesn't have to stay Kiosk-only. This becomes a general Manage page: standing, scope-targeted device state, as opposed to Actions' one-shot commands. Kiosk lock is the first policy; the charging-pad enable/disable and offline-unlock settings already work this exact way today (scope + standing state) and would move here next. Fleet Health folds in too — its score/verdict/crash signals already live on Overview's command-center section, so this dock slot is free for Manage instead of carrying a near-duplicate page.
