Graph time selector

The current picker is two datetime-local boxes you type into blind. This replaces them with a brush over a data-density timeline — you can see where the check-ins are and just drag a window over them. Presets set it instantly; drag the edges to fine-tune; slide it with the arrows. Semantic ranges (last night, today) map to real service hours.

Why this shape: people don't think in ISO timestamps — they think "the spike last night" or "during lunch." The density bars show them where data actually exists (no more selecting an empty range), the brush makes width and position one gesture, and semantic presets snap to the venue's real service window. The exact start/end are still there in the read-out for when precision matters.
