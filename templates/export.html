Columns

fixed

Every row is one check-in the device actually sent, at its own timestamp. The CSV carries:

  • serial_number, timestamp (UTC)
  • battery_pct — battery %
  • battery_temp_c — device temperature (°C)
  • charging — 0 or 1
  • wlc_state — wireless charging in words (vacant / charging / pad misplaced)
  • charging_pad — enabled or disabled for that device
  • ram_used_pct — RAM used %
  • storage_free_pct — storage free %
