What this serial is

{{.Serial}} is not a device serial. A T7 whose bootloader is corrupted starts without a serial on its boot command line, and Android reads the next item on that line instead — here the modem setting androidboot.baseband=msm. Every tablet in that state reports the same value, so the server cannot tell them apart.

Until 24 Sep they were merged into one "device" under this serial: several tablets' history interleaved on one record (19 different boots and 29 addresses). That record has been deleted, and the server now refuses this serial — nothing these tablets send is stored.

How to fix it

  1. Find the tablet at the site (below, where it probably is).
  2. Update its firmware with an OTA carrying MDM client 1.4.7 or later.
  3. It comes back as msm-<Android ID>, its own device with its own history, tagged Corrupted serial in the fleet. Re-flashing the bootloader restores the real serial.
