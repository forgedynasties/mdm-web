How devices get here

three paths, one fleet
1
Create a profile above
Device type, site and group are applied to every device that scans it. A profile with a site skips the inbox entirely.
2
Factory-reset device, tap the welcome screen 6×
Android opens the QR provisioning camera. No account may exist on the device yet.
3
Scan the profile's QR
The device downloads the agent, sets it as Device Owner, exchanges the token for its own credential and appears here within seconds.
1
Start from a clean device

Point it at this server
