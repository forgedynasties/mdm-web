{{/* ── How devices get here ───────────────────────────────────────────── */}}

How devices get here

four paths, one fleet
1
Open AIO Enroll on your Mac, Windows or Linux machine and sign in
Your dashboard account. The app gets its own enrollment token per person and device type, so every device it enrolls records who enrolled it — that is the "Enrolled by" on the device page and in the log above.
2
Plug in a clean device, or pair it over Wi-Fi
Factory-reset, with no accounts added, USB debugging on. The app lists what it sees and tells our hardware from lookalikes by serial.
3
Pick the device type and site, press Enroll
The app installs the standard client, makes it Device Owner and hands it its token over adb — the same steps as the adb path, without the terminal. The device shows up here within a minute.
1
Create a profile above
Device type, site and group are applied to every device that scans it. A profile with a site skips the inbox entirely.
2
Factory-reset device, tap the welcome screen 6×
Android opens the QR provisioning camera. No account may exist on the device yet.
3
Scan the profile's QR
The device downloads the agent, sets it as Device Owner, exchanges the token for its own credential and appears here within seconds.
1
Start from a clean device
Factory-reset or fresh, with no accounts added. Device Owner can only be set before an account exists.
2
One command per batch (kiosks without a camera, volume)
Plug the devices into a hub with USB debugging on. The script installs the agent, makes it Device Owner and hands it the server and a profile token. Nothing to type on the device. Use a profile with a device limit and revoke it after the batch.
tools/enroll-adb.sh -s {{.ServerURL}} -t enr_… {{/* token from a profile above */}}
3
By hand, if you prefer
adb install -r aio-mdm-dpc.apk adb shell dpm set-device-owner {{.AdminComponent}} adb shell am start -n {{.AgentPackage}}/.ui.MainActivity --es server_url {{.ServerURL}} --es enroll_token enr_…
{{if .HasAgentAPK}}The agent APK is at {{.AgentAPKURL}}.{{else}}Host an agent APK in Settings › App library first, or side-load your own build.{{end}}
1
Nothing to scan
Our own hardware ships with the system-app client. It authenticates with the fleet device key and registers itself on first check-in.
2
It lands in the inbox
Give it a site (and a product if the model default is wrong), press Done. That is the whole enrollment.
