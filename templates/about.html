      How it works

      A live line to every device.

      Each device keeps an open connection to the server, so commands land in seconds. If the network drops it, check-ins carry on over HTTP.

      On the deviceMDM client

      Reports health and runs commands.

      ConnectionWebSocket

      Always on, with HTTP check-ins as a fallback.

      In the cloudMDM server

      Stores telemetry, queues commands and updates.

      For your teamDashboard

      Overview, alerts, venues and releases in a browser.

      AIO hardware

      Firmware client

      Built into the Android image on AIO's own tablets and kiosks, so it can do what an ordinary app can't.

      • Firmware updates over the air
      • Lock to one app and reboot at system level
      • Updates itself as an app between firmware releases
      Off-the-shelf Android

      Standard client

      An app that becomes the device owner on POS terminals, kitchen screens and TV boxes from other makers.

      • Silent app installs and self-updates
      • Lock, reboot, wipe and config push
      • Remote screen, shell and full telemetry
