Known client behaviour (not bugs in this test)

  • battery_pct is the level the firmware broadcasts: BatteryService remaps 90–98% to 90–100% for display, so dumpsys battery (raw) can read 99 while the status bar and the MDM say 100. adb values are remapped the same way before comparing.
  • charger_voltage_mv is EXTRA_VOLTAGE — the battery voltage, not the charger's.
  • battery_temp_c is healthd's reading as-is (client 1.4.8+), graded against adb's; clients 1.4.6–1.4.7 reported a 3-minute average and will miss on every jump. RAM is cached 30 s, storage 120 s.
  • On battery with a healthy WebSocket the client polls every ≥5 min; charging edges and WLC changes are pushed immediately.
  • The server stores no device timestamp; this report times frames by uptime_seconds vs /proc/uptime.
