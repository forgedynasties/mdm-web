Pick: B, with A as the venue's view of the same data, and D as a later switch.

B puts the new work where enrolment already is and handles the real shape of the job — several venues, several boxes each, one admin at a desk. A is nearly free once B's table exists, and worth having because a venue's page should tell the truth about its Wi-Fi. C is the odd one out: it adds a screen to a guest-facing tablet to solve a problem the dashboard solves without a person in the room, and the T7 is the one device we'd rather keep guests from exploring. D is the destination but should wait until a few dozen B-enrolments have shown what sits on venue Wi-Fi in practice.
