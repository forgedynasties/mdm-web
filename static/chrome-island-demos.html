The top pill as an island, and a five-page dock

Two asks from the same session. One: the floating top pill should work like Apple's Dynamic Island — show what the page is about and its one or two actions, per page (the lab bar merging into it was the first case). Two: the dock should hold the five most important pages; Apps (now just the library), Lock (lock follows the role) and Alerts (the bell already has it, with "View all" now pinned at the top of its dropdown) come out. Values are examples.

Part 1 · What the pill shows

Pick a page to see what each option puts in the pill once the page has scrolled.

A · Context island my pick

Every page puts its name, its one-line status and its primary actions into the pill once you scroll past its own header. Live states (a lab session, a rollout in progress) colour the edge.

Good: the page's main buttons are always one click away, however far down you are — Reboot on a device, End lab, Promote on Releases. Pages that have nothing to say simply show their name.
Bad: every page has to declare what goes in its pill; the first version would cover the five dock pages, the device page and the lab view.

B · Live-only island

The pill stays plain, and only expands when something is happening: a lab session running, an OTA downloading on this device, a rollout in progress, a selection on Fleet.

Good: closest to Apple's idea — it changes only when there is news, so a change gets noticed.
Bad: on most pages most of the time it does nothing, and the everyday action (Reboot, Promote) is still at the top of the page.

C · Island from the start closest to today: plain brand pill

The page's title and actions live in the pill even before scrolling; the page itself loses its big heading row.

Good: one header per page, never two, and the most vertical room for content.
Bad: the biggest change — every page template loses its heading block, and a long page title has little room next to the brand and the bell.

My recommendation

Island A with dock 1. The pill becomes the place a page's main buttons always are, and the dock carries the five places people go every day. Lab view gets its timer and End lab in the pill, which is the merge you asked for.
