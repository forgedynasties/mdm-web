Venues and the venue page

Two pages. The venue page spends its first screen on three stat cards that repeat its own subtitle, then a 400px "Deploy devices" picker that is open all the time, then the Service hours and Guest Wi-Fi forms, and only at the very bottom the devices, which are what someone opens a venue to see. The Venues list still says "Fleet · Restaurants", repeats its headline in four stat cards, and shows each venue as a small card that fits only a name and a score.

In every venue-page option the picker becomes an Add devices button with a popover (shown open). The data is made up but has the shape of stage.
