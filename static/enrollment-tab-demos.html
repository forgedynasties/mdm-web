A · An "Enrollment" tab after About, admin-only Recommended

The card becomes a full-width tab in three columns: Identity, Settings, and Lifecycle (move, back to the fleet, retire, wipe) with the destructive ones in their own box. Shown only to admins, as the card is today. Good: the side column shrinks to Placement, Charging pad and Notes, which everyone uses; the rare, dangerous actions stop sitting next to everyday controls. Bad: one more tab, and an admin looking for "which server is this on" is one click further away.
