Overview — redesign options

The Overview reads as a personal dashboard (a greeting, a narrative sentence, an invented health score, coloured icon tiles, drag-and-drop widgets). Commercial MDM consoles read as operations tools: plain title, scope and time range, counts that link to lists, then the exceptions. Below: what the commercial products do, what specifically makes ours look home-made, the current page, and four options. All mock data is one consistent fleet: 438 devices across 31 restaurants.

Recommendation: D

Why D over the others. A looks professional but is still a gallery — the operator has to find the problem. B is the right first screen for on-call but thin for anyone else. C is the most AIO-specific but hides fleet-wide problems. D takes the strongest part of each, in the order the commercial consoles use, and keeps restaurants as the unit because that is how our support works.
