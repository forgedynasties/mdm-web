Header pill: how it should move

Same destination every time (flush bar → floating pill); only the motion differs. Two families: scroll-linked (the shape follows your finger: stop scrolling and it stops mid-way) and toggled (crossing a line starts one eased animation that runs to the end on its own). The first version shipped was a toggled 300ms flip, which is the "one large step". The counter bottom-left shows the pill progress 0–100%. Scroll inside each frame, slowly and fast.

1 · Scroll-linked, 160px, ease-out Live now

Progress = scroll ÷ 160px, eased so most of the change happens early. Good: never ahead of you. Bad: on a mouse wheel one notch is ~100px, so it still covers most of the morph in a single tick — which is what you saw.

2 · Scroll-linked, 400px, linear

Same idea, but the morph is spread over 400px of scroll with no easing: one wheel notch moves it a quarter of the way. Good: the most gradual; it feels like the bar is being pulled in by the page. Bad: on short pages it never finishes; the half-pill state is on screen a lot.

3 · Scroll-linked, two phases I'd pick this

First 0–100px: only the fill, ring and shadow come up — the bar "lifts" but keeps its shape. Then 100–320px: it pulls in and rounds. Nothing changes colour and shape at the same time, so no single tick looks like a jump. Good: the subtlest of the linked ones; the lift reads before the shrink. Bad: two things to tune instead of one.

4 · Toggled, slow settle (700ms, ease-out-expo)

Crossing 40px starts one animation: fast at first, then a long soft tail; back the same way under 40px. Not tied to scroll speed, so a wheel flick and a slow drag look identical. Good: always the same, always completes, feels "designed" rather than tracked. Bad: it is still a threshold; scroll to 41px and stop, and the bar keeps moving after you did.

5 · Toggled, soft corners only (500ms)

Smaller destination: the bar insets 12px a side and rounds to 22px, not a full 100px pill, with the lift. Less distance to travel means less to notice. Good: the calmest possible version; still clearly "floating" once scrolled. Bad: no longer a pill, so it doesn't match the dock's round ends.

6 · Scroll-linked, with inertia

Target progress comes from scroll (over 240px), but the bar chases it at ~10% per frame, so a wheel flick becomes a glide that settles over ~400ms, and a slow drag tracks closely. Good: no step can ever be larger than the glide allows, whatever the input device; still ends where the scroll says. Bad: a rAF loop runs while it settles (stops when caught up).
