Device page: Overview at full width — where Placement and Notes go

Base is A5, your pick: the top bar carries who and the main buttons, the tab row carries the vitals as chips. Now the left column goes too, so the Vitals chart gets the full width. The question is where Placement (restaurant, table, groups) and Notes live. Placement matters more than before: giving a device a restaurant is what makes it deployed. Values from your Kiosk 22.

P1 · Venue in the top bar, the rest in About my pick

The restaurant in the top bar becomes the control: click it to move the device (venue, table, groups in a small popover). Notes and the full placement move to the About tab, with the device's other facts.
