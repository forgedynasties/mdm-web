Compliance page

The new /compliance page from the plan. Each option answers the same three questions: how compliant are we, what is failing, and where. The numbers are live from 15 Oct where the server already knows them. Posture checks only count the 28 devices that report them, and everything else shows as unknown, which is the point of that state. Nothing is deployed yet. Fleet default: one baseline; lab devices and devices with no venue fall under a "Lab" override that only recommends.

Recommendation

C1, with C2 as its Venues tab and C3 as its Devices tab, so all three are in one page. Checks first, because what we change is the baseline and the fleet, while the venue view is mostly for the customer report, which gets its own design (compliance-report-demos.html, phase 4). One thing the numbers show either way: with 179 of 207 devices on no venue, "Assigned to a venue" would be the biggest failure, so the Lab override (recommended only) matters from day one.
