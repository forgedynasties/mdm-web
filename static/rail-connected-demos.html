Good at States the relationship outright: this rail is Fleet's. Useful when several pages have rails that look alike, because the join says which parent you are under.
Bad at Ornament that has to be maintained — the tongue must track the active item's position, and it breaks if the rail scrolls or the active item is off-screen.
What I would ship
A, with B's recess as a one-line option The real fix is the same in all three: the child rail becomes fixed and full-height like the app rail, flush against it, no card border, no radius, no gutter — and its collapsed state is a 56px column of initials in that same band, not a floating pill. A is that, with one hairline. If the two scopes read as too similar once it is live, B is a single colour change on top. C I would avoid: it is the most decorative and the first thing to break when a rail scrolls.
