T2 · Facts bar with labelled cells my pick

Tabs and Notes on the first row. Under them one bar of labelled cells, the way a spec sheet reads: what the device is doing (client, power, temperature, RAM, uptime, Wi-Fi), then where it is (restaurant, table, groups), each editable in place. The cells share the width evenly and wrap as whole cells on a narrow screen.
