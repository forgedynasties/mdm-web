Device header, round two: nothing heavy above the tabs

You liked A's direction but not the strip above the tabs. In all four below the top bar is the same as A (name, serial, status, build; Remote · Reboot · Open in lab), and what changes is where the rest goes — role and site, Name / Kiosk / Export, and the vitals (client, temperature, RAM, uptime, Wi-Fi). Device values are from your Kiosk 22 screenshot.

A4 · Tabs first; vitals open the Overview my pick

The page starts with the tabs. Role and site become tags in the island. Name, Kiosk and Export sit at the right end of the tab row. The vitals become the first card of Overview's left column — they are Overview content, and the other tabs never needed them.

My recommendation

A4. It removes the band above the tabs entirely, which is what bothered you, and the vitals become a readable "Right now" list beside the chart they explain. A7 is the fun one if you want the island to do more; it can be added on top of A4 later.
