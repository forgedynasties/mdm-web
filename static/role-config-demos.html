Role config: where "which app a role runs" lives

You asked for the role's app to come off Releases and onto a super-admin config page. Releases keeps the day-to-day work — new builds, columns, promote to prod — and shows the role's app read-only, with a "Change in role setup" link for super admins. The per-model firmware setting (Prod / Lab only / Off) is the same kind of decision, so it moves with it. The model → role mapping already lives on the admin-only Products page. Values below are examples.

A · Products becomes "Roles & hardware" my pick

One admin page answers the three set-once questions together: what each role runs, which models serve it, and whether each model takes firmware from us. Rows are grouped by role.

My recommendation

A. The three settings are one decision made once per role, and they already half-live on Products. Putting them together means a new model that enrolls as "role needed" is fixed in the same table that says what its role runs and whether it takes our firmware.
