Clients → devices list

Four ways to render the Devices table on the Clients page, mocked with the real firmware-qcom roster (36 devices: 9 behind, 27 that never reported a version, none up to date). Below them, two placements for the download / rollback actions the new per-digest APK archive makes possible. Pick one from each group.

The problem in all of this: the list is 36 flat rows sorted behind-first, more than two thirds of them no version rows that cannot be acted on at all, and a third of those have not been seen in over a month. The rows worth acting on are outnumbered 3:1 by rows that exist only to be scrolled past.

Group 2 — release rows, now that old builds are downloadable

The server now keeps every published APK under its own digest (data/agent/archive/<slot>/<sha256>.apk), so the Releases list can offer each past build as a download, and rolling back is a pointer flip instead of an AOSP rebuild. Two ways to put that on the row.

My pick: B + E. B because the Clients page exists to answer "what do I need to push, and to what?" — grouping puts the 9 actionable devices first and gives the other 27 a reason instead of a greyed-out button; the 14-day fold is what keeps a list that only grows from becoming unreadable. E because rollback is new and worth showing plainly: the first time anyone needs it will be during an incident, which is the worst moment to go looking in a menu. C is a good companion to B later — chips and search are additive — but on its own it leaves the default view exactly as crowded as it is now.
