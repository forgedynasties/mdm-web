The dock with names

The icons stay as they are: outline, turning solid on the page you're on, with the live figures on them (online ring on Fleet, spinner on Actions, count on Alerts). These options add the names back in different ways. Text costs width, so each dock is drawn at the page's full width (1,280px) and on a smaller laptop (1,000px), where the search field narrows to 220px as it does on the live site. Your three most-visited pages sit after the divider. Here they're Updates, Releases and Server.

Current: icons only Deployed

Names only on hover.

A · Name beside every icon

Every destination shows its name next to the icon, as the dock did before the search field. The current page gets a tint behind it as well as the solid icon.

Good at
  • Nothing to learn: every item says what it is.
Bad at
  • The widest. With the search field it needs about 1,200px, so on a smaller laptop the dock is wider than the page.

B · Name under every icon

Small names under the icons, like a phone's tab bar. Every item is labelled and the dock is only a little wider than icons alone.

Good at
  • Every item named, and it fits a smaller laptop with room to spare.
  • The same layout on phones, where it already stacks like this.
Bad at
  • 10.5px text is small, and the dock gets a few pixels taller.

C · Only the current page shows its name

Icons only, except the page you're on, which widens to show its name. Moving to another page slides the name across.

Good at
  • The narrowest option with text, and "where am I" is spelled out.
Bad at
  • You still hover to learn what the other icons are, which is the problem you want fixed.

D · Names on the four main pages, icons for the rest Recommended

Overview, Fleet, Actions and Alerts get their names. Your frequent pages and More stay icon-only, with names on hover, so they read as shortcuts rather than main navigation.

Good at
  • The pages everyone uses are named. The shortcuts that change per person stay compact.
  • Fits both widths, and keeps the old dock's split between "what everyone gets" and "what you built up".
Bad at
  • The frequent pages still need a hover the first time.

E · Every name, compact search

Every item keeps its name, and the search shrinks to a magnifier with ⌘K to make room. Clicking it opens the same search.

Good at
  • Every item named, and it fits a smaller laptop.
Bad at
  • The search stops looking like a search field, which is what made the search dock worth having.

Recommendation: D

D: names on Overview, Fleet, Actions and Alerts; icons for your frequent pages and More. The four pages everyone lands on are named, the search field stays a real search field, and it fits both widths. It also brings back the split the old dock had: named main pages, then quieter shortcuts after the divider. B is the pick if you want every item named. It's compact and matches the phone layout, at the cost of small text.
