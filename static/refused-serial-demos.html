Corrupted-serial tablets on the fleet page

A T7 whose serial reads corrupt (androidboot.baseband=msm) is refused by the server and stored nowhere — the refusal log (source address, build, attempts) is the only trace of it. The fleet page has to say so without shouting, and one click has to lead to what to do about it. All three options open the same detail view (bottom), whose most useful line is the likely location: the refused tablet's address matched against the devices already checking in from it.
