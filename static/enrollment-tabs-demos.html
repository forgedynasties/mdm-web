Recommendation

B for the page, and then the profile card question mostly disappears: on its own tab a profile has the full width, P1's card is fine there, and the QR goes back inside it. If you keep two columns instead (A or D), take P3 for the rail — a list with a ⋯ menu is the only shape that stays short no matter how many profiles accumulate. C is the compromise if you want tabs but refuse to split the two queues.
