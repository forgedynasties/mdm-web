Enroll page — the lopsided columns, and a header that stays

Two faults in what shipped an hour ago. The rail is far longer than the work column: profiles carry an inline QR (168px each), "Recent" holds 6 rows plus a search bar, and the four how-it-works panes are full step lists — against a left column that is one strip and a list capped at 8. And nothing stays put while you scroll: the appbar morphs into a floating pill at 40px but carries no page context, so once the title scrolls away there is nothing saying what page you are on or how many devices are waiting. Each option below is scrollable — try them.

The header, separately

All of B, C and D show the same sticky bar, because the fix is independent of the columns: the appbar already sticks and morphs to a pill at 40px — what it lacks is anything about this page. Filling it on scroll with the page name, the waiting count and the primary action (New profile) means the context never leaves the screen, and it costs one line of markup per page plus a class the existing scroll handler already toggles. If you would rather not touch the global appbar, the same bar can be a page-level sticky strip under it.

Recommendation

B, with C's sticky rail once the rail is short enough to fit. B removes the two things that make the rail tall — the inline QRs and the four step lists — and gives them the width they deserved anyway; after that the columns end within a card of each other, and pinning the rail is a one-line follow-up. D is the honest fallback if you decide the page should stop being two columns at all, but it undoes this morning's pick.
