Why

Once a device leaves the office,
we can't reach it. Unless…

The AIO MDM (Mobile Device Management) is our control room for every device in every restaurant.

It's how support sees a frozen screen in Las Vegas from the office, and fixes it, without a drive or a phone call.

It only works for devices that joined it. And the easiest place to join is here, in the office, before they're boxed up.

What the MDM does

What we can do once a device has joined

See it

Online or not, battery, Wi-Fi, where it is, which app is on screen.

Update it

Installs and updates the restaurant's app by itself. No one touches the device.

Lock it

Keeps the device on the restaurant's app, so guests and staff can't wander off into Settings.

Fix it remotely

See its screen, tap on it, restart it, from the office.

Get warned

Alerts when a device goes offline in service hours, overheats or keeps crashing.

Find it

Notices when a device leaves its restaurant, and can lock it if it's lost.

Which devices

Two kinds of devices

Nothing to do

Our own hardware

Tableside AI tablets (T7) and our Kiosk 18 / 22 / 27 panels.

The MDM is built into their software. Turn them on, connect Wi-Fi, and they join by themselves.

Use AIO Enroll

Devices we buy

POS terminals, kitchen displays, menu-board TV boxes, handhelds, payment terminals, ordinary tablets.

They come with plain Android, so they don't know the MDM. The AIO Enroll app introduces them. That's what we practise today.

Step 1 · the three rules

Get these right and the rest is easy

Fresh out of the box

Brand new is perfect. Used before? Factory reset it first.

Same Wi-Fi as your phone

The phone finds the device over the office Wi-Fi. Different network, no luck.

No Google account

Tap Skip at every sign-in screen during setup. Don't sign in to anything.

Why so strict?Android only lets the MDM become the device's manager on a clean device: no accounts, no other management app, one user. If any of these are there, the only fix is a factory reset. The app tells you which one.
