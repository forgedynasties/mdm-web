New Overview: header

The header has ten controls (the New / Classic switch and nine buttons), and they don't fit on one line. Visualize CSV wraps onto a second row and the "updated" time breaks under the title. Four ways to fix it are below. Each one is drawn twice. The first is at 1,280px, the widest the page ever gets: the content stops growing there, which is why it wraps even on a big screen. The second is at 1,000px, a smaller laptop, where wrapping gets worse. Buttons keep the same role rules they have now: locked for roles that can't use them, and Enroll / App library only for roles that have them.

Current Deployed

All ten in one row, right-aligned. At the page's full width they already need a second row.

A · Three buttons and a "More" menu

The switch, Fleet health, Alerts and Send action stay visible. Everything else moves into a "More" menu, drawn open in the laptop version.

Good at
  • Fits on one line at both widths, with room to spare.
Bad at
  • Deploy update and Enroll device are one click further away, and they are real daily actions for some roles.

B · Switch next to the title, actions + a "Tools" menu Recommended

The New / Classic switch changes the page, not the fleet, so it moves next to the "Overview" title. On the right are the actions you take on the fleet (Fleet health, Alerts, Send action, Deploy update, Enroll device), and a "Tools" menu holds the three that are about data, not devices (App library, Export, Visualize CSV).

Good at
  • One line at the page's full width. Below about 1,100px, Enroll device moves into Tools so it stays one line on a smaller laptop too. The time under the title stops wrapping.
  • Groups things by what they do: the switch is about the page, the buttons act on the fleet, the menu handles data.
Bad at
  • Export and Visualize CSV move behind a click, and so does Enroll device on a smaller laptop.

C · Label the actions, icons for the rest

The five fleet actions keep their labels. App library, Export and Visualize CSV become icon-only buttons with a tooltip.

Good at
  • Everything is still one click away.
Bad at
  • Three unlabeled icons that look alike (download, chart, grid) mean hovering to find the right one.
  • Still wraps on a laptop.

D · A toolbar row under the title

The title gets the full width, and the buttons move to their own row underneath, as a flat toolbar with the switch on the far right.

Good at
  • Room for all nine buttons at almost any width, with nothing hidden.
Bad at
  • Costs a row of height above the fleet.
  • A toolbar strip is a new pattern; no other page has one.

Recommendation: B

B, the switch next to the title and a Tools menu. It fixes both problems in the screenshot (the wrapped button and the wrapped time) at the page's full width and on a smaller laptop. It keeps the actions people use every day as visible buttons, and it puts the switch where it belongs: it changes the page you're looking at, not the fleet. A hides too much. C and D both still wrap on a smaller laptop, and D also adds height and a new pattern. Pick a letter and I'll build it.
