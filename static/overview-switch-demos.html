A quieter New / Classic switch

The two-button switch in the top bar is always on screen, and it's the loudest thing up there for something most people will set once or never. Below are quieter ways to offer the same choice. Each one works the same way in both directions: from Classic, the same place offers the new Overview. Your choice is still remembered either way.

Current: two buttons in the top bar Deployed

A · A setting in the account menu Recommended

"Classic Overview" becomes an on/off row in the account menu, next to your profile and theme. It's a preference, so it lives with the other preferences. Nothing is on the page itself.

Good at
  • Takes no room anywhere, on any page.
  • It's where people look for "change how the app looks for me".
Bad at
  • Hidden: someone who wants the old page back has to know to open the menu. The first-visit popup would say so once.

B · One line at the end of the Overview

A muted line under the last card: "Prefer the old Overview? Switch to Classic". On Classic, the same line offers the new one.

Good at
  • On the page it affects, but out of the way. The top bar stays clean.
Bad at
  • You have to scroll to the bottom to find it.

C · A small link on the first card

A faint "Classic view" link in the corner of the Fleet health card, like a "view source" link on an article.

Good at
  • Visible without scrolling, but small enough to ignore.
Bad at
  • It sits inside a data card, where it reads as if it belonged to Fleet health.

D · One icon button in the top bar

The two buttons become a single icon button the same size as the theme and alerts buttons, with a tooltip that says what it does.

Good at
  • One click, same place on both pages, and it matches the buttons next to it.
Bad at
  • An icon for "switch page version" isn't obvious. It needs the tooltip.

E · Only in the search

No control at all. Typing "classic" in the dock search offers "Switch to classic Overview" (and "Switch to new Overview" from Classic). The first-visit popup mentions it.

Good at
  • The quietest. It works from any page, not just the Overview.
Bad at
  • Only discoverable if you're told about it.

Recommendation: A, with E as well

A: a "Classic Overview" setting in the account menu, and the same switch available in the search (E), since adding it there is cheap. The choice is a personal preference that most people set once, so it belongs with the other preferences, not on the page. The first-visit popup already tells people where to find the old page, and it would point at the account menu instead. B is the pick if you want it on the Overview itself but out of the way.
