Flapping charger on the charge strip

A faulty charger (bad cable, port or supply) toggles charging every second or two. From the server change now on stage, history stores that stretch as one state, charging = 2, instead of a row per toggle — and firmware client 1.4.5 debounces it on the device too. The strip under the battery chart has to draw that state. You asked for a yellow strip; these are three ways to draw it, against what AT070AABU00509 looks like today. Same mock data in each: charging, a short spell on battery, 50 minutes of flapping, then charging again.

Today: green / grey churn Deployed

Every toggle is its own run, so the flapping stretch renders as a smear of green and grey slivers — and on a zoomed-out range it averages into what looks like normal charging. Nothing says the charger is faulty.

Option A
Solid yellow

The flap is one yellow run, exactly as asked. Simplest to build — a third fill colour in chargeStripPlugin.

Good atUnmissable, and it reads at a glance as "something else" next to green and grey.
Bad atColour alone carries it: for the ~8% of men with red-green colour blindness, this yellow and the charging green are close. It also sits near the wireless line's orange "pad misplaced".
Option B
Yellow with diagonal hatching Recommended

The same yellow, drawn as stripes. Hatching is the usual chart convention for "unreliable / fault" rather than a steady state, so it reads as a problem, not a third kind of charging.

Good atStill yellow, and the pattern tells it apart from charging without relying on colour, in both themes. No extra height, so the chart layout does not move.
Bad atSlightly more drawing code (a canvas pattern). At 6 px tall the stripes are fine-grained, so it reads as "textured yellow" rather than bold stripes.
Option C
Yellow, with a fault marker and rate

Solid yellow plus a small ⚠ marker where the flap starts, labelled with its rate. The chart itself says how bad it is.

Good atCarries the rate on the chart, and the marker survives a zoomed-out range where a short flap is only a few pixels wide.
Bad atA label on the chart competes with the build and command markers already drawn there; with several flaps in a day it gets busy.

Pick: B. It is the yellow strip you asked for, and the hatching keeps it readable for colour-blind operators without changing the chart's layout. In every option the hover readout names it — "Charger flapping · ~48 toggles/min" — and the legend gains one item. History recorded before the server change keeps its green/grey churn; only new flaps draw yellow.
