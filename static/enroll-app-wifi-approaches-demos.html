AIO Enroll — wireless: pair only when needed

Pairing and connecting are not a fixed sequence. In practice:

• Already paired (or the phone is trusted): just press Connect — no code, no pairing step.
• First time: Connect says it isn't paired, the panel opens Pair with a code, and after pairing most phones connect by themselves — the app waits for that before asking for anything.
• Sometimes it doesn't auto-connect after pairing: then the app says so and you press Connect.
• Connect fails for another reason: plain-language causes plus “Reset adb”.

This is how the app now works (built). Pick a scenario, then ▶ Play.

Option A — in the app now
One “Connect” step, pairing folded in built
Steps 1–2 are info (Wi-Fi, Wireless debugging on). Step 3 is Connect: phones found on the network are listed; pairing hides in a “Not paired yet?” section that opens by itself when it's needed.

Good: repeat users never see pairing; first-timers are walked into it exactly when needed; no fake “step 4”. Bad: pairing is a little less discoverable up front — hence the “Not paired yet?” link stays visible.

Option B — optional extra
“Switch to Wi-Fi” button on a USB-connected phone
For a phone that is already plugged in: one button in its panel runs adb tcpip 5555 and connects to the phone's own IP — no pairing, no codes, works on any Android version, and the fixed port avoids the “port changed” problem. It stays on Wi-Fi until the phone restarts. Not built yet.

Good: simplest path when a cable is at hand. Bad: an extra button on every USB device panel (only shown for Android devices on a cable).

Notes

• Phones found on the network also appear in the left rail (“Found on your network”) with a one-click Connect, so for paired phones the wizard isn't even needed.
• Failure help (port changed / different network / asleep / not paired / reset adb) shows under Connect in every scenario.
