Good: every value has a fixed place.
Bad: heavy: a boxed grid for seven short facts; health and placement look like the same kind of thing; with fewer values (a kiosk has no battery or Wi-Fi) the cells stretch wide and empty.

F1 · One quiet line, two groups my pick

No boxes. Under the tabs, one line: the device's state on the left as small label + value pairs, the placement on the right after a divider, each editable with ✎. It reads like a status line, not a form.
