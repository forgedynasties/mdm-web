Recommendation

B. It is the only one where the page's shape says what the page is for: the queue takes the width it needs for Site/Type/Done, the scout find states itself in one line, and profiles/history/help stay visible but stop competing. C is the boldest and I would not ship it — a sighting and an enrolled device are different things with different actions, and the merged table makes both worse. D is the cheap version of B, worth taking if you want the smallest diff rather than the better page.
