The Apps page — getting to the apps

Five blocks stack before you see a single app today, and two of them describe a page that no longer exists. Counts are the live fleet (187 devices): Nugget-uatv2 on 108 with 6 versions and 50 reporting none, stagev2 26, internal 15, Nugget 12, MPOS-UATV2 7 with no version at all.

Everything above row 6 is chrome, and a store is the one kind of page where the goods must come first. Four ways to fix it.

Layout A
One header line, findings collapsed to a strip

Smallest change: title + search + Upload on one row, the six findings folded into a single clickable strip, suggestions and the drop zone only when relevant. Grid starts immediately.

Deployed today: the six-block stack at the top of this page. I would ship B, and take D's copy fixes as part of it rather than as a separate step — the stale paragraph and the Actions button are wrong right now, whichever layout wins. B is the only one that both leads with the apps and tells the truth about the fleet's actual shape, and its "Needs attention" shelf is the findings panel doing its job without being a wall of banners. If a day is too much, A gets most of the benefit for an hour, and B can grow out of it later — the shelves are the same grid with a heading.
