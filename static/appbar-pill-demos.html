Header that turns into a pill · page backgrounds

Taken from deepseek.com: their header is a fixed, transparent bar that, 20px into a scroll, gains a translucent white fill, a 12px backdrop blur and a hairline border, with a 100px radius, so it reads as a floating pill the content slides under. Their page is warm off-white (#f9f8f8) with an animated "flow field" canvas behind the hero and 30%-white glass cards. Ours today: a full-width sticky glass bar, always on, flat #f7f7f7 page, white cards, and the dock already floats as a pill at the bottom. Scroll inside each frame to see the behaviour.

A · Pill on scroll I'd pick this

Full-width bar at the top of a page, like now. After 20px of scroll it pulls in to a centred pill (max 1140px, 100px radius), icons go round, brand shrinks a notch; 300ms ease. Good: top pill + bottom dock make a matched pair; the page edge shows through the gap on scroll. Bad: the bar must become position:fixed, so every page gets a fixed top padding; one more thing that animates while you scroll.

Page background

All four keep the tokens; dark theme shown with the toggle top-right.

1 · Flat Today

Plain --bg, white cards. Good: nothing competes with the data; cheapest to paint. Bad: every page looks the same from the top of the viewport down; nothing says "top of page".

2 · Warm paper + two soft glows I'd pick this

Page goes warm off-white (#faf8f5 — already the PWA theme colour), with a coral glow top-left and a faint blue one top-right, fixed to the viewport so they stay put while the page scrolls. Cards turn 62% white with a 10px blur so the glow shows through their edges. Good: the DeepSeek softness, pure CSS, no motion, pairs with the pill header. Bad: glass cards are one more blur layer; big tables stay opaque so it's only the card chrome.

Pita Place

3 · Flow-field canvas (what DeepSeek does)

A canvas behind the page draws slow particle trails along a noise field, at very low alpha. Paused when the tab is hidden, off under prefers-reduced-motion. Good: the closest copy, and it looks alive on the overview. Bad: a full-viewport canvas repainting at 30–60fps under a blurred header on a data-dense dashboard; the Actions page was already made faster by removing exactly this kind of work. Would need to be overview-only.

4 · Dot grid that fades out

A faint 18px dot grid at the top of the page that fades into the flat background by mid-viewport; cards as in 2. Good: an engineering feel that suits a device console; static, nearly free. Bad: busier behind tables than a glow; reads as a design tool rather than ops.

What I'd build: header A + background 2. A uses the same glass recipe the dock has, so the two pills match; 2 is the one background that adds warmth without any per-frame cost. The pill transition is a single is-scrolled class toggled from a passive scroll listener (20px threshold, rAF-throttled) — the same hook B and D would use. Combinations are fine: A+1, B+2, C+4 all work; 3 I'd only ship on the overview, if at all.
