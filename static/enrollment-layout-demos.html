Enroll page — the top of the page, and the inbox list

Two things asked about /enrollment: why the Fleet · Devices 30 · Restaurants 1 · Groups 1 · Enroll 15 block is there, and the Needs assignment card listing every device with no "show all" control.

What is actually wrong today

  • Two <h1>s on one page. fleet-tabs (layout.html:3405) emits <h1 class="ov-title">Fleet</h1>, then the page emits its own <h1 class="cc-h1">15 devices need a site</h1>. The convention is one h1 per page — so the generic one is the one to go.
  • The waiting count is on screen three times — the Enroll 15 tab pill, the Waiting KPI, and the card's own "15 waiting". Devices/Restaurants/Groups counts belong to the tabs they label; they are navigation, not this page's subject.
  • The inbox never caps. The card says data-lt-n="25", and the shared list JS (layout.html:1593) does cap at that — but revealing the rest needs a .lt-more element, and the inbox partial has none. So today: 15 rows all show, and past 25 rows the extras hide with no way to get them back. That is a bug, not just a missing button.

Recommendation
