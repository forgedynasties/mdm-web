Device graphs — layout demos

Four ways to lay out the metric charts, all rendering live (mock data). The duration buttons re-slice every chart; the overlay's legend chips toggle series; the focus rail's sparklines promote to the big chart on click. Flip light/dark top-right. Pick one and I wire it into the real Device Graphs card.

Design 1
Refined Tabs — one big chart, value on each tab closest to today
The current tabbed idea, tightened: each tab shows its live value inline so you read all four numbers without switching, and the active chart gets full height. Familiar, calm.

Design 2
Small Multiples — all four at a glance
No tabs at all. A 2×2 grid shows every metric at once, each with its own compact chart and live value. Best for spotting when two metrics move together (temp up, battery down).

Design 3
Unified Overlay — one chart, toggle the lines
Every series on a single chart (normalised 0–100 so they share a scale), with legend chips you click to show/hide each line. Powerful for correlation; click Temp + Battery only, ignore the rest.

Design 4
Focus + Sparkline Rail
One large focused chart, with a side rail of sparkline cards. Click a sparkline to promote it into the big view. Keeps a single clear hero chart while every other metric stays glanceable.
