Device wall — an icon per class, instead of a shape

What just shipped uses two shapes (square = battery, circle = mains) plus the tooltip for the exact class. This explores going further: a distinct glyph per class (8 of them — T7, kiosk, KDS, POS, mpos, payment, dongle, tablet) instead of grouping battery/mains into two buckets. Same SVG set used in the nav/device-type icons elsewhere in the app (#dk-t7, #dk-kiosk, etc. — simplified glyphs here).

Option B — same glyphs, shrunk to the "sm" 7px density
What Option A looks like past 900 devices
This is the actual risk with per-class icons: the wall drops to 7px squares once a restaurant/fleet view crosses 900 devices, and at that size every glyph below collapses into the same grey smudge — T7 and tablet are already indistinguishable, kiosk/KDS/POS blur into one blob. The two-shape version (square/circle) has no detail to lose and stays readable at any size — that's the whole reason it was picked over this.

Recommendation
Keep the shipped square/circle on the wall tile itself — it's the only option here that doesn't fall apart at the 7px "sm" density this view actually has to handle past 900 devices. If the goal is "I want to actually see a kiosk icon somewhere," Option C is the safe way to get that: real per-class glyphs in the legend, the device page's client pill, and the Apps/Commands picker — anywhere icon size is fixed and generous — while the wall's tiles stay exactly as they are today. Options A/B/D are here mainly to show why not: a full icon grid looks great at 14px and turns into noise (A) or a grey smear (B) the moment density forces 7px tiles, which this view does today.
