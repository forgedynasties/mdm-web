Sightings in the onboarding inbox — the decisions inside option B

Option B is picked: what a venue's T7 finds on the Wi-Fi lands on the Enroll page (/enrollment). Six smaller decisions remain, each with two or three ways to do it and a recommendation. Mechanics are section 7 of the plan. The data is the same throughout: AIO Test Bistro (scout AT070AA2600030), Bayside Pizza (scout AT070AABU00231), and the four lab devices plus a second HK1. Nothing shown is deployed.

1 · Where on the Enroll page

Today the page is: heading with the inbox count → four KPIs → Needs assignment (firmware devices and token-enrolled standard devices waiting for a site) → Enrollment profiles → Recent enrollments → How devices get here. Sightings differ from the existing inbox in one way that matters: "Done" on an inbox row is an instant database write, while "Enrol" on a sighting is a minute-long job on another device that can fail halfway.

3 · Watching it happen

An enrolment is eight steps on the scout, 30–90 s each device. The row must say where it is, and the whole venue must say how far along the batch is.

3A — Steps in the row, a bar in the venue header recommended

The row's select and button give way to the step list (the same eight the Enroll apps show). The venue header shows "1 of 2 done" with a thin bar and the device currently running. When a device finishes, its row turns green for a few seconds and leaves; the device appears in the venue's list.

4 · Blocked, stale and refused

Three kinds of device the scout sees but cannot or should not enrol. Each needs a different amount of screen.

4A — Blocked rows stay with the fix and "Check again"; stale rows fade; refused are a count recommended

Blocked (account, foreign Device Owner, second user): the row stays in the venue with the reason and the fix in plain words, and a Check again that re-probes just that device, because the fix is a factory reset someone does on site and then wants confirmed. Stale (not seen for a day): the row fades and says so; after a week it goes, and comes back if seen again. Refused our key: never a row, only a count in the venue header, because a device that refused us is not ours to list.

Picks: 1A · 2B · 3A (3B as history) · 4A · 5A · the three states in §6.

  • 1A because Enrol is a job and Done is a write; they should not sit in one list. The heading's second number is the only cost.
  • 2B because the sheet is the one moment a person confirms a class before it is on a live device; the dialog pattern already exists on the page.
  • 3A because the person who pressed Enrol should see the eight steps where they pressed it; the Commands page keeps the record.
  • 4A because the blocked row with Check again is what the installer uses after a factory reset; refused devices are a number, never a row.
  • 5A because "by whom, via what" is the same question for the Enroll apps, and the new column answers it for them too.

If these hold: build order is section 7.6 of the plan. First visible step on live is the §6 empty card with real scan counts and Enrol greyed — the read-only phase.
