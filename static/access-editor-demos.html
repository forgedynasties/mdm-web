Setting up someone's access

Today you set a starting point (everything, or nothing), then add allow and deny rules, each with a place and a list of about 17 actions. Both of today's "they can see everything" reports came from this: the right answer needed three separate settings to agree. The options below keep the same permissions engine and change how you describe them. The people are real, set up as they are on live today.

Exceptions (deny rules, time limits) sit under "More", out of the way. A preview on the right says, in numbers, what the person will get. Shown for Ali Hassan as he is today.

Recommendation

A, the two questions, with the people list. It removes the three-settings trap behind both of today's reports: "which devices can they see" is one answer, and it hides everything else, no switch needed. Every current setup maps onto it without changing anyone's access: Ali and Talmiz are "only these + look only", Shahrukh is "only these + custom", Tariq and demo are "every device + everything, remote included". Profiles (C) can come later if the team grows.
