Fleet map v2: a calmer zoomed-out map, and "away from venue"

You pointed out two things: the zoomed-out map is a mess, and there is no way to find devices that are not where their venue is. Mocks use the real tokens, so light and dark are accurate. Pick one from each of parts 1 and 2.

What went wrong in your screenshots. (1) Doubled numbers ("15" over "14", "36" over "35") are a bug of mine: when the live refresh redrew the map, the old cluster circles stayed behind under the new ones. Fixed in code, not deployed yet. (2) The world view exists because "fit everything" includes the office devices in Pakistan, so the US devices are squeezed into one corner and the rings pile on each other. (3) Dark centres on a dark map make the rings hard to read.

2 · Find devices that are not at their venue

A device has a position (from its Wi-Fi scan or GPS) and a restaurant has a position (the address on its page). If they are further apart than a limit, the device is "away from venue". Today that is checked by nobody.

89devices with a position
27within 200 m of their venue
61have no venue assigned (office and test devices)
0away from their venue right now

Those are today's real numbers. Three rules I would use: default limit 300 m, because a Wi-Fi fix is often off by 50 to 150 m and a large site can be 200 m across; devices with no venue are never "away" (they are listed as "no venue" so you can see how many are unplaced); and a limit you can change in Settings.

Recommendation

1A with 2A and 2B together, 2C afterwards. Soft discs plus "zoom to where most devices are" fix the world view, and the doubled numbers are already fixed in code. For the vicinity check, the map line (2A) is how you find them and the list filter (2B) is how you act on them (select, command, export). The alert (2C) is worth adding once you trust the limit. Building 2A+2B needs: venue coordinates in the map data (they exist: 27 of 28 placed devices have one), the distance computed on the server, a "Limit" setting (default 300 m), and one new filter value. No device data changes.
