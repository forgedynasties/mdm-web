Zero-touch enrolment — how an auto-connected device should look

The app now sweeps every address it can see on :5555 with the fleet adb key. A device whose image carries our key opens instantly (the T7 mock answers in ~100 ms); everything else is rejected or has the port shut. That is the vendor contract for devices we don't build ourselves — our key in the image, 5555 always open — and it means no pairing code at all for those devices. The screens below are three ways to say that in the list. Today these devices land under "Connected" with a Pair button, which is wrong twice over: nothing was paired, and the next thing to do is Enrol.

Pick one and I'll build it. Real data: AT070AA2600030 at 10.32.2.75, plus nine other devices on the Wi-Fi that refused the key.

Option A — a "Ready to enrol" section at the top smallest change

The list keeps its three sections but gains a first one: Ready to enrol, for devices that opened with the fleet key. Each row carries an Enrol button, so a device goes from appearing on the Wi-Fi to enrolled in one tap. "Connected" keeps its old meaning — devices you paired by hand with a code.

Option C — enrol the whole batch at once for a rollout

Aimed at the real job: a table of new devices from a vendor, all carrying our key. A banner at the top counts what is ready, you pick the class and restaurant once, and Enrol all runs them one after another with a line of progress each. Single devices still work the same way — the banner only appears when two or more are ready.
