Actions history — four ways to show it

The current history is a stack of tall rows with one big progress bar each. Here are four denser / clearer alternatives over the same data. Same actions, same statuses — different information shape.

Option A · Dense tablemost scannable
A proper table — columns you can scan down
Aligned columns (Action · Target · Result · Sent) pack ~2× more rows per screen and let the eye run straight down “Result”. The tiny segmented bar shows the exact done/failed/pending split at a glance.

Option B · Compact rows
One tight line per action
Everything on a single row: status dot, icon, action, payload, a target pill, and the result + time on the right. No bars — just a status colour and a count. Maximum density; great when history gets long.

Option C · Segmented cards
Two-up cards with an exact result breakdown
Keeps a card feel but two per row, and the bar is segmented — green/red/grey show the real done/failed/pending split with a numeric legend, not just a single fill. Good when the outcome mix matters more than raw speed of scanning.
