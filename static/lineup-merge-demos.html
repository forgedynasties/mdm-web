Releases + Hardware: one page

Both pages already have the same shape (the roles in a side list, one role at a time on the right) and answer two halves of one question: what is this kind of device (which models, their firmware mode) and what does it run (its app, the builds on prod and in lab, its MDM client and firmware). Merging puts everything about a role in one place. The data is from stage; the role shown is Tableside AI.

The name

LineupThe word the business already uses for the device range (POS, kiosk, KDS, menu board, Tableside AI). Short, fits the dock. my pick
Product managementYour suggestion. Clear, but long for the dock and it sounds like a team rather than a page.
RolesMatches the side list exactly; but "role" is also the dashboard's word for user permissions.
ProductsShort; but AIO's products are also what it sells to restaurants, so it is ambiguous on the dashboard.

M1 · One pane per role, three sections my pick

The side list is the roles (plus MDM clients and "Not attached" at the bottom). The pane for a role reads top to bottom: App (the app it runs and its builds: new, prod, lab variants), Hardware (the models attached, with "+ Add model" and each model's firmware mode) and Firmware (the firmware lane per model of ours). One URL per role: /lineup?role=t7.

My recommendation

M1, named Lineup. A role is one thing to the business, so one pane that says what it runs and what it is removes the back-and-forth between two pages that share a side list today. Hardware setup changes rarely, so it sits under the app where it does not get in the way of releasing.
