When staff take a device out of kiosk

A technician can leave kiosk on the device (five Power presses, then the exit PIN or the device's rotating code). What you decided: the device then stays out of kiosk until someone locks it again, whether or not a kiosk rule covers it, and the Kiosk page and the device page say so. Today an exit leaves almost no trace: the server turns kiosk off, and from then on the device page just says "Kiosk off", as if it had never been locked; under a kiosk rule it is even locked again within a minute. Three pieces below: the wording in the rule editor, the Kiosk page, and the device page. On live today no device is in kiosk and no rule exists, so the examples are made up: a Nugget rule at Flights Vegas.

3How staff get out

The same on every device; nothing to set here.

  1. Press Power five times within 10 seconds. A keypad opens over the app.
  2. Enter the exit PIN, or the code shown on the device's page in the MDM. Both work with no connection.
  3. The device stays out of kiosk until someone locks it again here. The Kiosk page and the device's page show that it was taken out.

A · A strip at the top of the Kiosk page Recommended

Whenever a device has been taken out of kiosk on site, the page opens with it: which device, where, when, what it was locked to, and two answers. "Lock again" puts it back to its rule (or its own setting). "Leave it out" keeps it unlocked and clears the notice; under a rule it becomes an exception, like a device changed by hand. The rule's row also counts it.

Recommendation

Editor: the new wording. Kiosk page: A, the strip. Device page: A, the notice, plus the amber Kiosk button. An unlocked kiosk is something to act on, so it should be in front of whoever opens either page, with the two answers right there. "Lock again" puts the device back to what locked it (its rule, or its own setting); "Leave it out" says that is fine and, under a rule, makes it an exception you can see on the rule's device page. Skip the alert for now: add it if exits turn out to go unnoticed.
