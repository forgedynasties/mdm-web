Device page menus

The device page has 11 dropdowns and popovers, and almost every one has its own CSS: isl-menu, cpill-pop, dd-pad-pop, dd-notes-pop, pl-pop, vt-menu and cr-pop. They also open in two different ways (details and JS-toggled hidden). That is why they look unrelated. The client menu shouts in bold caps and its changelog runs off the edge. The pad popover's toggle is a big orange pill. The ⋯ menu mixes a form with menu items and still says "Kiosk", although the feature is now Lock. Each option below redraws the same four menus: client, charging pad, ⋯ more and chart range. Whichever option is picked becomes one shared kit, also used by the venue, group, notes and date menus.

Recommendation

M1. These are menus and small toggles, not documents, so they should open where you clicked and close as fast. One kit (.cc-menu, .cc-mi, .cc-msec, .cc-msep, .cc-mfoot) and one way of opening fit the "one vocabulary per job" rule. It replaces the seven private popover styles on this page, and the account and fleet menus can follow later. Changes that come with it: "Kiosk" becomes Lock; the client menu stops listing clients you can't switch to; changelog entries lose the "client 1.9.8 (code 81):" prefix, since the version is already in its own column.
