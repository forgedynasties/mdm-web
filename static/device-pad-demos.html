Device page: the charging pad

The wireless-charging pad on a T7 needs two things on the page: whether it is on (the switch) and what it is doing (phone on it, vacant, misplaced). Today that is a whole card, about 180px, mostly empty. The battery line in the graph is already coloured by the pad state. Four smaller ways; the T7 from your screenshot, pad misplaced.

W3 · A slim one-line strip, only when something is wrong

When the pad is fine, nothing is shown except "Pad" in the facts line. When it is misplaced or unreadable, a single-line strip appears under the facts with the reason and the switch.
