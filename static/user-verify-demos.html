Verifying people by hand

Someone who signs up with an email and password cannot sign in until they click the link in the verification email. If the email never arrives, or goes to spam, they are stuck and nobody on the dashboard can help: "unverified" is only a word in grey on the People list. Two people were stuck like this on 30 Sep: anwar.ahmed@aioapp.com (signed up 29 Sep, as "Anwae Sheikh") and Haris Azhar (30 Sep), both viewers; they were verified by hand in the database that day. Every earlier @aioapp.com signup clicked its link within minutes, so the email stopped arriving between 23 and 29 Sep. The options below let a super admin verify them. Only the super admin: verifying an account is vouching for who owns that address.
